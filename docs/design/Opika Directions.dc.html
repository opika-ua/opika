Opika · design directions · turn 1

Three answers to the same tension

Each card below shows the identical hardest case: a fostered animal (city only, no coordinates), last confirmed 41 days ago, rabies vaccination confirmed by the state registry, spay/neuter not recorded. The directions differ in who carries the uncertainty — the record, the shelter, or the interface itself.

1A Запис · The Record

Civic-document logic, in the register of Diia and the state pet registry. The listing is a dated record, and a record that hasn't been touched in 41 days is simply a record with a date on it. Uncertainty is neutral metadata, never a warning.

ПАЛІТРА
Paper white, one institutional blue, greys for everything unrecorded. No red, no amber — nothing in the palette can be read as an alarm. Dark mode inverts to near-black paper.
ТИПОГРАФІКА
e-Ukraine for UI, e-Ukraine Head for names, a mono for all metadata. Metadata in mono is the whole trick: it reads as recorded, not as claimed.
ФОРМА
Radius 2px, hairline 1px rules, one hard offset shadow for stack depth. Fields sit in labelled rows; nothing floats.
РУХ
160–200ms, cubic-bezier(0.2,0,0.4,1). No overshoot, no bounce. Rotation coupling 3° max. The card leaves the way a page turns.
Trust it projects: procedural. The platform looks like it keeps records, and a record with a visible gap is more credible than one without. Reads as serious to grant reviewers and to shelters handing over listings.
Where it's weak: an animal becomes a row in a register. Warmth has to come entirely from the photo and the name, and shelter photos are bad. Risks feeling like a municipal database of dogs — cold at exactly the moment someone is deciding to love one.
1B Голос притулку · The Keeper's Voice

The uncertainty is spoken, in first person, by the shelter. Every freshness state becomes a sentence with an author and a date attached. The app is not a database; it is a relay for what a named person last said.

ПАЛІТРА
Unbleached paper, one leaf green for anything a third party has verified, warm browns for everything unrecorded. Dark mode is lamplight, not black.
ТИПОГРАФІКА
A Cyrillic serif for the shelter's words and the animal's name; a humanist sans for controls. Quoted speech is set in the serif so it reads as testimony, not UI copy.
ФОРМА
Radius 12–20px, photo inset inside a paper margin, soft low shadow. Rounded, held-in-the-hand objects; nothing edge-to-edge.
РУХ
280–340ms, cubic-bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1). Slow settle, no spring past target. Rotation 6°. Cards are set down, not flicked.
Trust it projects: human accountability. Somebody named is answerable for the gap, and admitting it plainly is disarming. Warm without sentimentality; the shelter looks careful rather than negligent.
Where it's weak: unscannable. Freshness in prose can't be compared across a deck, filtered, or read at a glance, and every string needs the shelter to write it or the platform to ventriloquise them — which is a real honesty risk. Ukrainian at +15% makes these quotes long, and warmth reads as softer rigour to a grant reviewer.
1C Певність · Confidence Surface

The interface itself carries the uncertainty. Every card renders at a confidence level: a measured field at the top edge, and known facts held at full strength while unconfirmed ones are visibly held back. Nothing is hidden and nothing is dressed up — the card just declines to look as sure as it isn't.

ПАЛІТРА
Almost no hue at all: confidence is encoded in luminance, so the same scale works in light and dark and for colour-blind users. Colour is reserved for the reveal moment, which is the only place it appears.
ТИПОГРАФІКА
One variable Cyrillic sans across the whole app, plus mono for measured quantities. Weight and opacity do the work colour usually does — but never below 4.5:1.
ФОРМА
Radius 6–8px, no shadows at all — stack depth comes from luminance and scale. A 4px measured field spans the top edge of every card as a fixed structural element.
РУХ
220ms, cubic-bezier(0.33,1,0.68,1), spring-back at stiffness 320 / damping 30. During drag the confidence field is the only thing that moves independently — it brightens as the card commits, so the gesture reads as asking, not scoring.
Trust it projects: epistemic. The product's differentiator is legible in three seconds without reading a word, and it holds up under scrutiny: the interface is structurally incapable of overselling a listing. Cheapest at 60fps and the most accessible of the three.
Where it's weak: it looks like an instrument. A stale card is visibly dimmer, which shelters may read as a penalty for being slow — and with a thin feed, a mostly-grey deck can look broken or abandoned rather than careful. Warmth has to be fought for everywhere.
Reference by id in chat: 1a, 1b, 1c. All three refuse red/amber for staleness, keep a full button path beside the gesture, and treat unknown as “не записано”, never as “no”.
