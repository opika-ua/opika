  fit; the sort switch stays right-aligned on the first line. Same component and behaviour as the

  the group names are his approved kabinet titles (PC1-D2), the options are PR 1's card words
  (his PC-Q2 plural for temperament, the neuter colours he confirmed, PC1-D4).
