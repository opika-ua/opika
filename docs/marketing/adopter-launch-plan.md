# Adopter launch plan

**Date:** 2026-10-06 · internal, English · **Status:** plan structure, not a schedule
**Constraints (handoff §8):** no launch date, no budget, one person at ~10 h/week. Phased by
**verified-shelter count — 1, 5, 15** — never by weeks. No calendar dates appear in this document
on purpose; outreach (H6) is Oleksii's and starts only after the rehearsal gate in
`docs/handoff-2026-10-04.md`.
**Inputs:** `competitive-brief.md`, `brand-voice.md`, `brand-review.md`, `brand-improvements.md`.
**Binding:** the eight commitments in `docs/standing-constraints.md` and handoff §3. Every
message and tactic below was checked against them; where one bites, it's named.

**Explicitly out:** paid ads of any kind, influencer budgets or paid posts, giveaways and partner
incentives, adopter accounts, notifications, email capture, any "save this animal" or alert
feature (none exist), Instagram as Opika's own channel at launch, and any number about the
registry that isn't true on the day it's said.

---

## 1. Campaign overview

**Working name:** «Дата на кожній картці» — internal label only, not a slogan.

**One sentence:** make Opika the place a Kyiv-oblast adopter lands when a shelter's own Telegram or
Instagram post links to an animal — and the place they come back to when OLX and reposts have made
them unsure what's real.

**Primary objective.** For each phase, reach a steady state where **every published animal has
been opened by adopters and at least some animals have had their contact revealed** — i.e. the
pipe from a shelter's channel to a direct message works for every shelter in the registry. Targets
are set per phase in §7 as *learning thresholds*, not growth targets, because there is no baseline
and inventing one would be the fabricated proof the brand forbids.

**Secondary objectives.**

1. Every shelter in the registry shares its animal links in its own channels without being chased.
2. Per-animal and per-city pages start receiving Google traffic (the only compounding channel).
3. Zero listings of animals that are already home (the trust-killer `forShelters.whenAnimalFindsHome`
   names).

**The real constraint is supply, not demand.** Adoption demand already exists (OLX's free-animal
category alone drew ~50k responses in its own 2023 count). The plan's pace is set by how many
shelters Oleksii can verify and keep current at 10 h/week — so every phase gate is a *shelter* gate.

## 2. Audience

**Primary — adopters in Kyiv oblast towns, then Kyiv city.**
> Someone in Brovary, Irpin, Bucha, Bila Tserkva or Vyshhorod who wants a dog or cat, has scrolled
> OLX listings mixed with breeders, clicked Telegram reposts with dead links, and missed Instagram
> stories that expired. Their question: *is this animal real, still available, and is this a real
> shelter?* They find things through local Telegram chats, the shelter's own posts, and Google.

- **Stage:** consideration → decision. They already want an animal; they lack a trustworthy list.
- **Device:** mid-range Android on mobile data (design target).
- **Why the oblast first:** the city has Kyiv Digital's municipal service; the towns have no
  organised, web-linkable, verified registry (competitive brief §2).

**Secondary — Kyiv city adopters.** Reached by spillover (search, shares). Not targeted head-on;
Opika is a neighbour to Kyiv Digital, not a rival.

**Enabling audience — the verified shelters.** Not the campaign's target, but its distribution:
each shelter's existing followers are the first adopters. Shelter outreach itself is a separate,
later handoff (handoff §10); this plan only covers what shelters are *asked to share* once they're in.

## 3. Key messages

All Ukrainian lines are *proposals* for Oleksii to write or approve. English is the sense.

**Core message.**
*Animals from shelters in Kyiv oblast that a person checked — each with the date its information was
last updated, and the shelter's own contact.*
*proposal:* «Тварини з перевірених притулків Київщини. На кожній картці — коли інформацію востаннє
оновлювали, і контакт притулку напряму.»

| Supporting message | Adopter pain it answers | Proof (mechanism, not numbers) | Commitment it must respect |
|---|---|---|---|
| **«Перевірений» means a person checked.** Registration + bank details + a reference, or a real visit/call + two references. | "Is this a real shelter or a seller?" | The page that says exactly what was checked (`forShelters.verification`) — link to it | Don't inflate to "certified", "partners", "ongoing control" |
| **Every card says when it was last updated.** | "Is this animal still available?" | The pips + day count on every card; the shelter's own sentence on how often they update | #7 — "updated", never "confirmed" |
| **You write to the shelter yourself.** No form, no queue, no one in between. | "Will I wait days for a reply from a middleman?" | The reveal screen: «Притулок не знає про цей запит, поки ви не напишете самі.» | #4 — never "we connect you" |
| **Nothing here is paid for.** Order is by date; no shelter can buy a higher place. No money passes through the site. | "Is this an ad?" | `whatHappensToAnimals`: «Це все, що впливає на порядок.» | #1, #5 |
| **One person runs it, independently.** Not a state or city service. | "Who is behind this?" | `/pro` + the non-affiliation line (brand-improvements #1) | #6 — «я» |

**Message variations by channel.**

- *Shelter's own post* (their voice, not Opika's): the shelter writes about the animal as they
  always do; the link is the only Opika element. Opika supplies **no caption copy for shelters to
  paste as if it were theirs** — that would be Opika speaking for them (#4). It may supply a
  one-line *optional* explanation of what the link is, clearly the shelter's choice to use.
- *Local Telegram community post* (Opika's voice, «я»): one paragraph — what this is, which towns,
  what «перевірений» and the date mean, a link to the city page. No animal photos as bait.
- *Search* (per-animal and per-city pages): the page's own title/description — already written in
  the catalogue.
- *Press/blogger*: the mechanism story (§4.4).

**Words this campaign never uses:** match/«пара», «ідеальний друг», «терміново», «останній шанс»,
«рятуйте», «N людей уже…», «сотні тварин», «платформа», «ми». Full list: `brand-voice.md` §4.

## 4. Channel strategy

Four channels, all owned or earned, all zero-cost. Effort is per phase, in Oleksii-hours.

### 4.1 The shelters' own Instagram and Telegram — *primary distribution*

- **Why:** the adopters who already follow a shelter are the highest-intent audience that exists,
  and the shelter's voice carries trust Opika doesn't have yet. Competitive brief §3.2: these are
  distribution, not competition.
- **What Opika provides:** each animal's stable URL with a working link preview (requires the OG
  work in brand-improvements #5 — **a launch dependency**); a one-page "how to share a link" note
  for shelters (Telegram: paste link; Instagram: link sticker in stories, link in bio — Instagram
  posts don't carry clickable links).
- **What Opika never does:** write captions in the shelter's name, ask shelters to post on a
  schedule, report back to a shelter how many people opened its links or took its contact (#2 —
  this includes aggregates like "your page got 40 views this week"), or rank shelters by activity.
- **Effort:** low ongoing; ~2 h once to write the sharing note.

### 4.2 Kyiv-oblast Telegram communities — *Opika's own voice*

- **Why:** town chats and channels (per-town news, "lost & found" pets, volunteer chats) are where
  oblast adopters already ask "where can I get a dog in [town]?" — and where the dead-link problem
  lives.
- **How:** one honest post per community, **only after that town has at least one verified shelter
  with live animals** (otherwise the link lands on «Під ці фільтри зараз нікого немає»). Ask the
  admin first; many chats ban promotion. Link the **city page** (`/tvaryny?misto=<slug>`), not a
  single animal. Reply to direct questions in the thread as Oleksii; never DM members.
- **Opika's own Telegram channel:** opens at phase 2 (brand-improvements #9 for its rules). Content
  = new animals' links, with each shelter's consent, plus short mechanism explainers. Comments off
  or pointing to shelters.
- **Effort:** medium — ~1 h per community (finding admin, asking, posting, answering), capped at a
  few communities per phase.

### 4.3 Organic search — *the only compounding channel*

- **Why:** a per-animal page that ranks for «взяти собаку Бровари» or a city page for «притулок
  Ірпінь тварини» keeps working without Oleksii's time. Numbered, indexable pagination and city
  slugs already exist for exactly this reason (`docs/stack-decision.md`).
- **How:** lift `noindex` with the first real shelter (see decision D1 below); register in Google
  Search Console; submit a sitemap; make sure per-city pages have a unique, factual title and
  intro line each. No keyword-stuffed copy — the voice guide applies to SEO text too.
- **Not now:** `/seo-audit` runs after launch (handoff §10).
- **Effort:** ~3 h once (Search Console, sitemap check, city titles), then ~½ h/month reading
  Search Console.

### 4.4 One local-press or blogger angle — *once, at phase 3*

- **The story:** not "new app helps animals" (every outlet has run that), but the mechanism —
  *a registry where the date on every card means exactly what it says, run by one person, with no
  paid placement and no middleman.* It's specific, checkable, and true.
- **Target (pick one):**
  - **Option A — a Kyiv-oblast town news site or Telegram channel** (reaches adopters where the
    shelters are; local outlets need local stories). *Lean.*
  - **Option B — a Ukrainian tech outlet** (e.g. dev.ua has covered pet-adoption tools before):
    reaches developers and potential collaborators, not adopters.
- **Rules:** facts only as of the day of the interview, dated; no adoption numbers unless real and
  the shelters agree; never "Tinder for pets" — if the journalist reaches for it, offer the
  mechanism line instead; never compare with Kyiv Digital.
- **Effort:** ~4–6 h (pitch, interview, fact-check what they publish).

### Channels considered and rejected

| Channel | Why not |
|---|---|
| Paid social / search ads | No budget; also handoff §8 |
| Influencers / pet bloggers | Budget, and the register (cuteness, urgency) clashes with the voice |
| Opika's own Instagram | Competes with its own shelters' channels; rewards what the voice forbids |
| Facebook groups (Opika posting) | Possible later for older adopters; one channel at a time at 10 h/week |
| OLX | Posting Opika links as listings would be spam on someone else's marketplace |
| Offline adoption days | Kyiv city runs them; not Opika's job at MVP |
| Email / push to adopters | No accounts, no notifications — and none are planned for launch |

## 5. Phase plan — by shelter count

Each phase has **entry criteria**, **what to do**, and **exit criteria**. Moving on is a decision,
not a date.

### Phase 0 — Before the first real shelter (now → rehearsal passed)

**Entry:** now. **Shelters:** 0 real; demo corpus behind the pre-launch gate.

| # | Item | Depends on | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.1 | Critical copy fixes C1–C5 (brand-review) | Oleksii's Ukrainian | 1–2 h |
| 0.2 | Non-affiliation line (brand-improvements #1) | — | ~1 h |
| 0.3 | Favicon / icons (brand-improvements #4) | — | 3–4 h |
| 0.4 | OG images without relative dates (brand-improvements #5) | 0.3 for the mark | 6–10 h |
| 0.5 | Name decision written down (brand-improvements #2) | — | 2–4 h |
| 0.6 | Measurement baseline: the SQL queries in §7 saved as files, a simple manual log (spreadsheet) for adoptions | — | ~2 h |
| 0.7 | Confirm whether Vercel Web Analytics breaks page views down by `?misto=`; if not, rely on the DB-side city metric | — | ~½ h |
| 0.8 | Shelter "how to share a link" note (§4.1) | 0.4 | ~2 h |

**Exit:** rehearsal passed (engineering handoff row 10); D-7 demo-to-real transition written; 0.1–0.4
done.

### Phase 1 — One verified shelter

**Entry:** one shelter verified and onboarded; demo corpus fully removed from production
(`REGISTRY_HAS_NO_REAL_SHELTERS` false — **never mix demo and real animals**).

**Decision D1 — when to lift `noindex`.**
- *Option A — lift at shelter 1.* Starts the SEO clock (indexing takes weeks); pages are already
  honest about being small («Притулків тут небагато… це нормально»). Risk: a near-empty registry is
  what a Google visitor sees.
- *Option B — lift at shelter 5.* Registry looks fuller on first impression; SEO starts weeks later.
- **Lean: A** — the copy already handles smallness truthfully, and search is the only channel that
  compounds.

**Do:**
- The shelter shares its animals' links in its own channels, in its own words (§4.1).
- No Opika-voice promotion anywhere yet. No Telegram community posts. No press.
- Watch the whole pipe end-to-end: shared link → page view → reveal → (shelter-reported) message →
  adopted → listing updated.

**Exit:** the shelter has kept listings current for a sustained stretch without chasing; at least one
animal has gone home and been taken off the list promptly; the pipe has been seen working; Oleksii's
time per shelter per week is known (this sets the pace of phase 2).

### Phase 2 — Five verified shelters

**Entry:** five shelters live, ideally spread over at least three towns.

**Do:**
- Each shelter shares as in phase 1.
- Open **Opika's Telegram channel** (rules in brand-improvements #9).
- First **Kyiv-oblast Telegram community posts**, one per town that has a live shelter (§4.2).
- Search Console: check which per-animal and per-city queries appear; adjust city titles if a
  town's page is invisible.
- First shelter-reported time-to-adoption entries in the manual log.

**Exit:** every shelter has had reveals on its animals (internal metric, never reported to them);
at least two towns produce search impressions; maintenance (adopted removals, updates) still fits
inside 10 h/week alongside onboarding.

### Phase 3 — Fifteen verified shelters

**Entry:** fifteen shelters live.

**Do:**
- The **one press/blogger angle** (§4.4). Now there's enough real supply that a reader who clicks
  through finds a registry, not a demo.
- Telegram community posts in any remaining towns with a live shelter.
- The first time an aggregate sentence about the registry may be used publicly — only a *count of
  shelters and animals*, true on the day, dated: *proposal* «Станом на <дата>: N притулків,
  M тварин.» Never anything about adopter attention.
- Decide whether to add Facebook groups as a second Opika-voice channel (older adopters).

**Exit:** this is launch's end-state. Hand over to a steady-state plan (and `/seo-audit`) once the
registry has run at this size for a while.

### What changes if H2 (shelter self-serve accounts) ships before outreach

The engineering handoff schedules H2 before the rehearsal. If shelters can log in and mark animals
adopted themselves at launch:

- Commitment #6's «я внесу все сам» / «напишіть, і я приберу» copy changes **in the same release**
  — and so do messages in this plan that lean on "one person does the work". "One person runs it"
  stays true; "I enter everything for you" may not.
- A real "still looking" confirmation (handoff row 10) changes what the freshness date can say —
  commitment #7 gets re-read then, not before.
- Re-run the brand-review checklist against the new copy before phase 1.

## 7. Success metrics — only what the site can actually measure

### Available today

| Metric | Source | How |
|---|---|---|
| **Page views by path** (gallery, per-animal, `/prytulkam`, `/pro`) | Vercel Web Analytics (already installed) | Dashboard. Event URLs include the query string; confirm the dashboard groups `?misto=` values before relying on it for per-city views (0.7) |
| **Referrers** (Telegram, Instagram, Google, direct) | Vercel Web Analytics | Dashboard — the clearest signal of which channel works |
| **Contact reveals** — total, per animal, per shelter, per city | Postgres `reveals` (`adopter_id`, `animal_id`, `shelter_id`, `revealed_at`) | Saved SQL; city via the animal's city. `adopter_id` is a device, not a person |
| **Distinct revealing devices** | `reveals` | `COUNT(DISTINCT adopter_id)` — a floor/ceiling-uncertain proxy for people |
| **Reveals per published animal** | `reveals` ⋈ `animals` (`listing_kind IN ('published','reserved')`) | Saved SQL — finds animals no one opens (a listing-quality signal) |
| **Deck engagement** | Postgres `swipes` | Secondary; the gallery is primary |
| **Supply health: freshness distribution** | `animals.last_updated_at` → fresh / aging / stale share | Saved SQL — the leading indicator of trust erosion |
| **Animals marked adopted** | `animals.listing_kind = 'adopted'` | Count. There is **no adoption timestamp** in the schema today |
| **Time to adoption** | Manual log: listing `created_at` + the date the shelter reports | Spreadsheet, per animal, shelter-reported |
| **Search impressions / clicks per page** | Google Search Console (after `noindex` lifts) | Free; set up in phase 1 |

### Not measurable — don't claim it

Whether a reveal led to a message, a visit, or an adoption (only the shelter knows); unique people
across devices; anything about why someone didn't adopt. Product analytics (PostHog) is deferred by
the engineering handoff until there are users.

### Learning thresholds per phase (not growth targets)

| Phase | Primary: pipe works | Supply health | Channel signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Every published animal has page views; at least some have reveals | No animal listed after the shelter says it's home | Referrers show the shelter's channel |
| 2 | Every shelter has reveals on its animals | Stale share stays a minority; adopted removals happen promptly | Telegram + Google both appear as referrers; Search Console shows impressions for ≥ 2 towns |
| 3 | Reveals spread across most towns, not concentrated in one shelter | Same | Search is a meaningful share of referrers |

Real numeric targets get set **after phase 1**, from phase 1's own data. Setting them now would be
guessing.

### Guardrails (watch every phase)

- **Reveal data is internal only.** Never shown or reported to any shelter, in any form, including
  aggregates (commitment #2). The engineering register notes the data and index already exist —
  this is the place it leaks by accident.
- **Stale share rising** → talk to the shelter before adding more shelters.
- **Any report of an animal "already taken"** → fix that day; it's the failure
  `forShelters.whenAnimalFindsHome` calls the one that makes every card untrustworthy.
- **Oleksii's weekly hours** on maintenance vs onboarding → if maintenance crowds out onboarding,
  stop adding shelters.

**Reporting cadence:** a short monthly note to self (or the project) — the table above, filled in.

## 8. Budget

None, by design. All channels are owned or earned. Time is the budget: phase 0 ≈ 18–26 h of
brand/launch work (items 0.1–0.8) on top of engineering; phases 1–3 are paced by per-shelter
onboarding and maintenance hours, measured in phase 1. Hosting costs are engineering's, not
marketing's.

## 9. Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| **Thin supply makes the registry look empty** | High early | Copy already says so honestly; Telegram community posts only where a town has live animals; D1 decision made consciously |
| **A shelter stops updating** — stale cards, then "already taken" calls | Medium | Freshness distribution as a leading indicator; talk to the shelter early; a shelter can leave at any time (`howToLeave`) and that's fine |
| **Oleksii's hours run out at ~5–10 shelters** | High | Phase gates are shelter gates; measure per-shelter time in phase 1; H2 self-serve is the structural fix |
| **Mistaken for a state/city service, or for a copy of Kyiv Digital** | Medium | Non-affiliation line before phase 1; never compare publicly; different geography and mechanism stated plainly |
| **Telegram community admins treat posts as spam** | Medium | Ask first; one post per community; link a city page, not an ad; reply as a person |
| **Press frames it as "Tinder for pets"** | Medium at phase 3 | Offer the mechanism line; ask to fact-check the description; if the frame lands anyway, don't amplify that piece |
| **Reveal stats leak to a shelter** (well-meant "your animals got 30 contacts") | Medium once H2 admin exists | Treat as a commitment breach; keep it out of every shelter-facing surface and message |
