## STOP REASON
The fix for finding 1 means choosing a login-CSRF mechanism (IN-T7, sign-in security) that fits Oleksii's verbatim `Referrer-Policy: no-referrer`. Options:
- **(a) Accept `Sec-Fetch-Site: same-origin`.** Also accept `Origin` equal to our own; refuse anything else, including a null Origin without Sec-Fetch-Site. Keeps no-referrer exactly as specified. Cost: a browser that lacks Fetch Metadata (Safari before 16.4) cannot sign in.
- **(b) `Referrer-Policy: same-origin` on the confirm GET.** The Origin check then works everywhere, and the token goes only to our own POST as Referer, never cross-origin. Departs from the owner's literal header.
- **(c) A double-submit CSRF cookie set on the GET.** A new cookie with its own attributes (STOP item 5).

Recommended: (a). Whichever is chosen, add a harness test: real Chromium, GET the link, click «Увійти», assert a 303 and the session cookie. That test would have caught this.

Needed from Oleksii: pick (a), (b) or (c), and confirm or amend the no-referrer requirement if (b).
