## FINDINGS

1. **[High]** `registry-invitations.ts:205` / `kabinet-invitation-repo.ts` `resend`: resend brings an expired invitation back to life without running `roleOfAddress`, so the IN-Q8 state can still be reached.
   - Failure scenario: invite olena@ to A. After 72h, invite her to B (allowed, since A's has expired). Then `resendInvite` A's. Result: two live pending invitations in two organizations. Accepting the second hits `auth_member_user_id_unique`.
   - Evidence: a probe in registry.test.ts (since removed) printed `PROBE 200 200` with two `pending` rows. A repo-level probe printed `live pending invitations: 2 2 orgs`.
   - Fix: resend runs `roleOfAddress({shelterId})` and refuses. That means declaring `ADDRESS_HAS_A_ROLE` on `registry.admins.resendInvite` too, which is a contract addition. Name it in the PR body.
2. **[Med]** The Q8 mapping was deferred, and the deferral is only in the commit body. Oleksii's "not this PR" covers the partial index, not the mapping.
   - I judge the deferral acceptable. A mapping with no insert behind it is scaffolding, and today it is vacuously "never a 500".
   - But it stays reachable after this commit:

     - two concurrent invites, or `create`s, to different shelters. Both pass the pre-check, and no index stops them until 0012.
